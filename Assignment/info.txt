- i++ is not applicable in the python, instead of it, we use i=i+1
- most efficient code of prime and not prime number check with all edge cases covered
n = 2
i=2
flag=0
while(i<n):
  if(n%i==0):
    flag=1
    break
  i=i+1
if(flag==0 or n<2):
  print(f"{n} is prime")
else:
  print(f"{n} is not prime")
